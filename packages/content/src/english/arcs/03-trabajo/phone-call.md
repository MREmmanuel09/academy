# Episode 3.4: The phone call you didn't want to take

**Duration:** ~14 minutes
**Target level:** B1
**Setting:** Maria's desk. The phone rings. It's a client she doesn't know well.

---

## Characters

- **Maria** — Answering.
- **James** — A new client from a fintech startup. Fast talker, no patience.
- **Tom** (briefly) — On chat, helping Maria in real time.

---

## Scene 1: Answering the phone (4 min)

**[Audio: phone ringing, picked up]**

> **Maria:** "Hello, Lumen Marketing, Maria speaking."

> **James:** "Hi, is this the project manager for the Acme account?"

> **Maria:** "Yes, that's me. How can I help?"

> **James:** "Great. Look, I'm in a rush. We've got a launch on the 12th and I need to know if the landing page will be ready. We're a week behind and I'm starting to panic. Can you give me an update?"

> **Maria:** "Of course. Let me pull up the project. One moment, please."

> **James:** "Sure."

**[Maria on chat to Tom]**

> **Maria (chat):** "Acme client on the phone. Wants to know about the landing page. What do I say?"

> **Tom (chat):** "Landing page is on track for Friday. The hero copy is approved. Images are being reshot. Don't promise images earlier than Friday."

## Scene 2: Saying no to a client (5 min)

> **Maria:** "Thanks for waiting. The landing page is on track for delivery on Friday. The hero copy is approved. The images are being reshot — those will be ready by Friday too."

> **James:** "But we need the images earlier than that. The launch is on the 12th. That's not enough time to test."

> **Maria:** "I understand the concern. Let me check with the photographer. Can I call you back within the hour?"

> **James:** "I'm in meetings all afternoon. Just tell me now — can we get the images by Wednesday?"

> **Maria:** "I don't want to promise something we can't deliver. Let me get a firm answer from the photographer, and I'll email you by 5 PM today. Is that okay?"

> **James:** "… Fine. But 5 PM. Not 5:01."

> **Maria:** "Understood. 5 PM."

### Frase clave

**"I don't want to promise something we can't deliver."** — Forma educada de decir "no" sin decir la palabra.

Variaciones:
- "Let me get a firm answer before I commit to a date."
- "I'd rather check and come back to you than give you the wrong information."
- "I want to make sure we set you up for success, so let me confirm first."

> **Tom (chat):** "That was perfect. 'I don't want to promise something we can't deliver' — clients actually respect that more than a fake yes. He'll tell his boss you were honest."

## Scene 3: The follow-up email (5 min)

**[Audio: typing]**

> **Narrator:** "After the call, Maria writes a follow-up email. This is non-negotiable in UK office culture: if you said you'd do something on a call, write it down. Otherwise, you didn't say it."

```
Hi James,

Thanks for the call just now. As discussed:

- Landing page delivery: Friday
- Hero copy: approved
- Images: I'll confirm timing with the photographer by 5 PM today

I'll send the photographer's update before 5 PM. If we can't get images earlier than Friday, I'll explain why and propose an alternative.

Best,
Maria
```

> **Tom:** "Two reasons we do this. One: memory is unreliable, even on calls that just happened. Two: it gives the client something to forward to their boss. Your email becomes their internal ammunition. You want them to have it."

### Frase clave

**"As discussed"** — Frase estándar al inicio de un bullet list que resume lo hablado en una llamada o reunión. Indica: "esto es lo que acordamos".

Variaciones:
- "Following our call…"
- "To confirm what we discussed…"
- "Per our conversation…"

### Modismos del episodio

- **"In a rush"** — Con prisa.
- **"Starting to panic"** — Empezando a ponerse nervioso.
- **"Set you up for success"** — Poner las condiciones para que te vaya bien.
- **"Just now"** — Justo ahora, hace un momento.
- **"Not 5:01"** — Forma humorística de enfatizar puntualidad exacta.
