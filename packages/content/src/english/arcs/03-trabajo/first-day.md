# Episode 3.1: First day at the office

**Duration:** ~15 minutes
**Target level:** A2
**Setting:** A marketing agency in Shoreditch, London. Maria's first day. 9:00 AM.

---

## Characters in this episode

- **Maria** — Two months in London. Just started her new job as a project manager.
- **Tom** — Her team lead. British, late 30s, friendly but direct. Uses sarcasm.
- **Priya** — Designer on the team. Indian-British, mid 20s, helpful.
- **Receptionist** — Older British man, formal.

---

## Scene 1: Reception (3 min)

**[Audio: office lobby, soft music, printer]**

> **Narrator:** "It's Maria's first day at Lumen Marketing. She arrives at the office at 8:55 AM. The receptionist greets her."

> **Receptionist:** "Good morning. Can I help you?"

> **Maria:** "Yes, hello. I'm Maria García. It's my first day. I'm starting as a project manager."

> **Receptionist:** "Of course. Welcome to Lumen. Could I see some ID, please?"

> **Maria:** "Sure. Here you go."

> **Receptionist:** "Thank you. Let me just check you in on the system. … There you are. Maria García, project manager, starting today. Could you sign here, please?"

> **Maria:** "Of course."

> **Receptionist:** "Wonderful. Your team is on the third floor. The lift is just over there on your left."

> **Maria:** "Thank you very much."

> **Receptionist:** "You're welcome. Have a great first day."

## Scene 2: Meeting Tom (5 min)

**[Audio: door opening, office chatter]**

> **Maria:** "Hi, I'm Maria. I'm looking for Tom?"

> **Priya:** "Oh, you must be the new project manager! I'm Priya. Welcome. Tom is just finishing a call. Can I get you a coffee while you wait?"

> **Maria:** "Yes, please. That's very kind of you."

> **Priya:** "How do you take it?"

> **Maria:** "With milk, no sugar, please."

> **Priya:** "Coming right up."

**[Tom arrives, slightly hurried]**

> **Tom:** "Maria! So good to finally meet you in person. Welcome to the team. How was your first week in the UK? Settling in okay?"

> **Maria:** "Yes, thank you. Everyone has been very welcoming. I'm still finding my way around, but it's going well."

> **Tom:** "Brilliant. Well, let me give you the quick tour. We're a small team — about 12 people. We mostly work with tech startups. Your main project will be a campaign for a new fitness app. I'll introduce you to everyone at 10. Any questions so far?"

> **Maria:** "Not really. But I do have a small one — where's the kitchen?"

> **Tom:** "Ha! Very important. Through there, second door on your right. There's also a coffee machine if you don't want to bother Priya."

> **Priya:** "I heard that."

### Frase clave

**"How do you take it?"** — Forma natural de preguntar cómo alguien quiere su café (con leche, con azúcar, etc.).

Variaciones:
- "How do you take your coffee?"
- "How would you like your coffee?"
- "Any milk? Sugar?"

## Scene 3: The morning standup (5 min)

**[Audio: meeting room, people sitting]**

> **Tom:** "Right, let's make a start. Maria, this is our morning standup. We do it every day at 9:30. It's quick — five minutes max. Each person says what they did yesterday, what they're doing today, and if anything is blocking them. You'll see, it's simple. Priya, do you want to start?"

> **Priya:** "Sure. Yesterday I finished the mockups for the fitness app. Today I'll be working on the icon set. Nothing blocking me."

> **Tom:** "Great. … Tom: I had three calls with the client yesterday. Today I'm following up on the contract. I'm waiting on legal to come back to me. … Maria, you don't have to say anything today. Just listen and get a feel for it. Sound good?"

> **Maria:** "Sounds good."

> **Tom:** "Cool. Right, that's us. Let's get to work."

### Gramática: presente simple vs. presente continuo

En este episodio escuchas ambas formas mucho:

- **Presente simple** (rutinas, hechos): *"I take my coffee with milk."* / *"We have a standup every day."*
- **Presente continuo** (acciones en curso ahora): *"I'm working on the icon set."* / *"I'm following up on the contract."*

Truco para español: presente continuo ≈ "estoy + gerundio" en español. Presente simple es como el presente español pero sin la extensión temporal.
