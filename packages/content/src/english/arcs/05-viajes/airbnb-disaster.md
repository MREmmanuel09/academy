## Scene 2: First call to the host (3 min)

> **Maria:** "Hi, is this Robert? I'm at your flat — the one I booked for the weekend."

> **Robert:** "Oh, hi! Yeah, the one on Cowgate. How's it going?"

> **Maria:** "Not great, honestly. The window is cracked, the fridge doesn't work, and the bathroom has mould."

> **Robert:** "Oh, sorry to hear that. I can come round tomorrow and have a look?"

> **Maria:** "Tomorrow? I'm here for the weekend. I need this sorted today."

> **Robert:** "Look, it's a Saturday. I'm a bit tied up. Monday I could come?"

> **Maria:** "Monday I'll be gone. This is not what the photos showed. Can I get a refund?"

> **Robert:** "I'd have to think about that. I can't just refund on the spot."

### Cultura: hosts en Airbnb UK

> **Narrator:** "Robert is a typical 'set-and-forget' Airbnb host — he lists the flat, takes the money, and avoids dealing with problems. This is unfortunately common. In the UK, the platform (Airbnb) is responsible for the booking, not the host. So if the host won't help, you escalate to Airbnb support. The customer service line is 24/7. They have the power to refund, reassign, or compensate. Use them."

---

## Scene 3: Calling Airbnb (5 min)

> **Lina:** "Airbnb customer support, this is Lina. How can I help?"

> **Maria:** "Hi, I checked into a flat in Edinburgh today. It's not what the photos showed. The window is cracked, the fridge doesn't work, there's mould in the bathroom. The host won't help until Monday but I'm leaving Sunday."

> **Lina:** "I'm so sorry to hear that. Can I have the booking reference?"

> **Maria:** "It's HM4X7PQ2."

> **Lina:** "Thank you. Let me look... I see the booking. I'm very sorry, Maria. That doesn't match our standards. Let me see what we can do."

> **Maria:** "I'd like a full refund, please. And maybe help finding somewhere else to stay tonight?"

> **Lina:** "Of course. I can issue a full refund — you'll see it back on your card in 5-7 business days. For tonight, I can book you into a hotel nearby — also covered by us. Will that work?"

> **Maria:** "Yes, that's perfect. Thank you so much."

> **Lina:** "You're welcome. I'm sending you a confirmation email now. Anything else?"

> **Maria:** "No, that's all. Thanks again."

### Frase clave

**"I'm so sorry to hear that"** — Expresión estándar de empatía al recibir una queja. Se usa en customer service, en el trabajo, en la vida. No es solo "lo siento", es "lamento mucho que te haya pasado eso".

### Cultura: customer service en UK

> **Narrator:** "UK customer service has a particular style. They are trained to be polite, empathetic, and solution-focused. The phrases you'll hear:
> - 'I'm so sorry to hear that'
> - 'Let me look into this for you'
> - 'What I'd like to do is...'
> - 'Will that work for you?'
> - 'Is there anything else I can help you with?'
>
> The key is: they want to solve your problem, not win an argument. The customer is not always right, but the customer is always the customer. Use this to your advantage when something goes wrong."
