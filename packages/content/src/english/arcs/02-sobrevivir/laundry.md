### Grammar note: modal verbs for advice and ability

> "You **can** use the 'whites' setting, or even 'hot wash' if they're very dirty."
> "I**'ll** do the whites on 'whites' and the rest on 'mixed'."

| Modal | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| can | ability / possibility | "I can swim." / "You can use this machine." |
| could | past ability / polite request | "I could swim when I was 5." / "Could you help me?" |
| may | permission | "May I use the bathroom?" |
| will / 'll | intention / future | "I'll do it tomorrow." |

"Can" is also used for general possibility: "You can buy this anywhere."

### Grammar note: present simple for habits and opinions

> "Londoners **can** seem cold, but they **are** very helpful when you need it."
> "Everyone **wants** to help with directions."

We use present simple for:
- Habits: "I go to the gym every day."
- Permanent truths: "Water boils at 100 degrees."
- Opinions: "I think London is great."
- General facts: "Londoners love tea."

### Grammar note: "as soon as" + present for future events

> "As soon as it **finishes**, take everything out."

In time clauses with "as soon as," "when," "before," "after," "while," "until," we use **present tense** for future meaning:
- "When I **see** him, I'll tell him." (NOT "When I will see him")
- "Call me as soon as you **arrive**."
- "Don't go until it **stops** raining."

This is a tricky rule for Spanish speakers. In Spanish, you can use future in time clauses ("cuando llegue"), but in English, you use present.

## Cultural note: launderettes in the UK

> Most flats in London don't have washing machines. People either use a **launderette** (self-service) or have a **washer-dryer** installed in the kitchen. Some launderettes also offer a "service wash" — they wash, dry, and fold your clothes for you.

> **Detergent** is usually included in the price at launderettes, or available for a small extra cost. You don't need to bring your own.

> The "high heat" setting is usually 60-80 degrees, "low heat" is around 40 degrees. Hot washes can shrink clothes, so be careful with wool and cotton.
