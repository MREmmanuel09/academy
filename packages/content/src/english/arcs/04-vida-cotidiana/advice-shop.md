# Episode 4.4: Asking for advice

**Duration:** ~13 minutes
**Target level:** B1
**Setting:** Maria is unsure about renewing her rental contract. She asks for advice from Sam, Tom, and her landlady.

---

## Characters

- **Maria** — Considering renewing or moving.
- **Sam** — Has rented in London 10 years. Practical advice.
- **Tom** — Work perspective on commute vs rent trade-offs.
- **Mrs. Patel** — Maria's landlady. 60s, Indian-British, kind.

---

## Scene 1: Maria asks Sam (4 min)

> **Maria:** "Sam, can I ask you something?"

> **Sam:** "Course. What's up?"

> **Maria:** "My contract's up in two months. I don't know whether to renew or move. What would you do?"

> **Sam:** "Hmm. Depends. How much is the rent going up?"

> **Maria:** "About 8%."

> **Sam:** "Ouch. That's a lot. Where are you thinking of moving to?"

> **Maria:** "Somewhere cheaper, maybe. Further out."

> **Sam:** "OK, my two pence — the rent savings might not be worth it once you factor in the commute. How far's your current place from work?"

> **Maria:** "Twenty minutes."

> **Sam:** "Yeah, I'd stay. Twenty minutes is gold in London."

> **Maria:** "Really?"

> **Sam:** "Mate, I had a two-hour commute once. It nearly killed me. I quit the job, kept the flat."

### Frase clave

**"My two pence"** = mi opinión modesta. "Two pence" (dos peniques) = una cantidad mínima. La idea es "solo digo lo mío, no soy experto".

Variaciones:
- "If you want my two cents..." (US)
- "Just my opinion..."
- "I'd say..."

---

## Scene 2: Maria asks Tom (3 min)

> **Maria:** "Tom, can I pick your brain for a sec?"

> **Tom:** "Sure, go for it."

> **Maria:** "What do you think about living further out to save on rent?"

> **Tom:** "Honestly? Depends what you value. If you want to save money and don't mind a longer commute, go for it. If you value your sanity, stay where you are."

> **Maria:** "That's what Sam said."

> **Tom:** "Smart man, Sam. He's not wrong. A long commute eats into your evening. You get home tired, you don't cook, you don't see friends, you don't exercise. The savings add up to nothing if you stop living."

> **Maria:** "Wow, that's... actually quite persuasive."

> **Tom:** "Just my two pence."

### Frase clave

**"Pick your brain"** = pedir la opinión / experiencia de alguien. Literal: "escoger tu cerebro". Forma coloquial y amistosa.

- "Can I pick your brain about marketing?"
- "Wanted to pick your brain on something."

**"Just my two pence"** = "es solo mi opinión modesta". Mismo uso que antes.

## Scene 3: Maria asks her landlady (3 min)

> **Maria:** "Mrs. Patel, can I ask you something?"

> **Mrs. Patel:** "Of course, dear. Come in, sit down. Tea?"

> **Maria:** "I'd love one, thank you. So, the contract — I'm thinking about whether to renew. I was wondering if there was any flexibility on the price."

> **Mrs. Patel:** "Hmm. I can do 5% instead of 8%. That's the best I can do, love."

> **Maria:** "Really? That's much better than I expected. Thank you."

> **Mrs. Patel:** "You've been a good tenant. Quiet, pays on time, doesn't complain. I want to keep you happy."

> **Maria:** "That's so kind. I really appreciate it."

> **Mrs. Patel:** "Now, shall we have that tea?"

### Cultura: negociar el alquiler en UK

> **Narrator:** "Mrs. Patel just offered a 3% discount, no questions asked. Why? Because Maria has been a good tenant — pays on time, no complaints, no drama. In the UK, landlords value reliability above all. If you've been good, asking for a reduction is normal. If you've been difficult, don't even ask."

> **Maria's voiceover:** "I was so nervous to ask. I thought she'd say no. But she was lovely about it. And she said she'd rather keep a good tenant than get a few extra pounds a month."

### Modismos del episodio

- **"Course"** = "of course" abreviado, muy UK. "Course I will."
- **"Mate"** = amigo, colega. UK, muy informal.
- **"Shall we...?"** = "queremos...?", "qué tal si...?". Suave, cooperativo.
- **"Just my two pence"** = "es solo mi modesta opinión".
- **"Pick your brain"** = pedir consejo / experiencia.
- **"To be a good sport"** = ser buen perdedor, no quejarse. (Aparece en otros episodios)
- **"Love" / "Dear"** = formas cariñosas de dirigirse a alguien, muy UK.

## Gramática: modals of advice en práctica

> **Sam:** "I'd stay."

Pattern: `would + base verb` = consejo suave. "Yo me quedaría."

- "I would go for it." (yo lo haría, lo intentaría)
- "I would take the offer." (yo aceptaría la oferta)
- "I would think about it." (yo lo pensaría)

> **Tom:** "Just my two pence."

Pattern: "Just" al inicio para suavizar. "Solo / simplemente..."

- "Just my opinion." (solo es mi opinión)
- "Just wondering." (solo me preguntaba)
- "Just saying." (solo digo / conste que lo digo)

> **Mrs. Patel:** "I want to keep you happy."

Pattern: "to want to + base verb" para expresar intención.

- "I want to help." (quiero ayudar)
- "I want to make sure..." (quiero asegurarme)
